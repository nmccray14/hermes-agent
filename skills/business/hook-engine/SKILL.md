# Hook Engine

## Overview

This is the single default hook system. Use it before writing or approving any hook-like opening across No Stranger, Founder Operator, ads, landing pages, organic content, YouTube, email, SMS, and social.

A hook is not a slogan. A good hook creates a reason to keep reading/watching by combining a specific person or scene, real tension, a believable promise, and a clean next beat.

This skill combines:

- The archived No Stranger hook optimizer: strict proof, compliance, product facts, paid/organic routing, and humanization.
- Corey Haines / marketingskills patterns: angle generation, channel structure, performance iteration, social/video opening discipline, and copy clarity.
- Noah/Rook taste gate: no generic formula junk, no contradiction, no fake proof, no AI-polished abstractions.

## Mandatory Trigger

Load and apply this skill before writing, editing, judging, ranking, approving, or improving any:

- Hook, headline, subheadline, landing page hero, prelander hero, PDP hero.
- YouTube intro, title angle, thumbnail line, Founder Operator opener, talking-point lead.
- Static ad headline, Meta primary-text first line, video ad first 1-5 seconds, first frame, text overlay, UGC opener.
- TikTok/Reels/Shorts opening line, carousel slide 1, X/LinkedIn first line, newsletter/social lead.
- Email subject/preview, SMS first sentence, popup headline.

If the user asks for `hooks`, `headline help`, `opening`, `first line`, `first frame`, `thumb stop`, `scroll stop`, `intro`, `hero`, `subject line`, or `make this less generic`, use this skill.

## Core Standard

Every final hook must pass six gates:

1. **Specificity** — names the exact person, moment, object, pain, or contrast. Avoid category labels like `busy people`, `ecom brands`, `high performers`, or `wellness journey` unless grounded in a concrete scene.
2. **Tension** — makes the viewer feel a problem, contradiction, gap, curiosity, or useful disagreement immediately.
3. **Proof/source** — does not imply evidence we do not have. Label the source pattern when needed: account signal, customer language, public source, founder POV, external swipe, product fact, or untested hypothesis.
4. **Humanization** — sounds like a real person would say it. Short, spoken, concrete. Remove AI polish.
5. **Continuation fit** — the next beat actually pays off the hook. No bait-and-switch, no contradiction between hook/body/offer/destination.
6. **Channel fit** — matches the placement: YouTube intro is not a TikTok opener; landing-page hero is not a Meta static headline; email subject is not a product tagline.

## Default Workflow

1. **Identify placement.** Choose one: `landing_page`, `pdp`, `prelander`, `youtube_longform`, `founder_video`, `short_form_video`, `static_ad`, `video_ad`, `email`, `sms`, `social_post`, `carousel`, `script_intro`, `thumbnail_title`.
2. **Load live context if available.** Use project files, source docs, product facts, audience notes, performance data, transcripts, comments, reviews, or public sources before guessing.
3. **Define the job.** What must the hook earn? Click, scroll stop, watch first 60 seconds, read the page, understand the offer, reply, or buy.
4. **Pick angle families.** Generate from 3-5 different families, not 15 synonyms of one idea.
5. **Write rough hooks.** Prioritize substance over polish.
6. **Run the taste gate.** Kill generic, contradictory, overclaimed, vague, over-clever, too-polished, or channel-wrong hooks.
7. **Humanize.** Make the best hooks sound spoken and specific.
8. **Recommend one winner.** Do not just dump options. State why the recommended hook wins and where to use it.

## Angle Families

Use these as source patterns, not formulas to copy blindly.

### 1. Specific Pain Scene

Best for cold traffic, short-form, founder content, landing-page heroes, and ads.

Pattern: exact moment + audience identity + consequence.

Examples:

- `You trained before sunrise, ate clean, then still hit a wall by 2:17.`
- `The order issue was not hard. It just sat there for 36 hours because nobody owned it.`

### 2. Contrarian Reframe

Best when the audience blames the wrong thing.

Pattern: common blame target is not the real issue.

Examples:

- `Your workout might not be the problem.`
- `Most AI agents do not need more autonomy. They need a smaller job.`

Avoid lazy `Unpopular opinion:` unless the line after it is genuinely sharp.

### 3. Proof / Mechanism Challenge

Best for supplements, landing pages, PDPs, static ads, and category education.

Pattern: make the viewer understand why the mechanism, dose, workflow, or source matters.

Examples:

- `If the dose is buried, that tells you something.`
- `The boring workflow is the one that pays.`

### 4. Warning Without Fearmongering

Best when the market is doing something sloppy or expensive.

Pattern: stop doing the category mistake, then show why.

Examples:

- `Stop buying ashwagandha by vibes.`
- `Do not hire an AI agent to run your store. Use it to catch dropped balls.`

### 5. Founder / Operator Confession

Best for Noah, Brent, founder-led content, long-form intros, and organic trust-building.

Pattern: honest founder pain + hard-earned fix.

Examples:

- `I was crashing at 2pm. So I built what I could not find.`
- `I do not want AI running my business. I want it catching the work humans keep missing.`

### 6. Visual-First Contrast

Best for static ads, first frames, thumbnails, carousel slide 1, and video openers.

Pattern: visual contrast + 3-8 word line + concrete label.

Examples:

- generic 150mg blend bottle vs. studied 500mg dose bottle.
- messy app stack vs. one workflow that catches order issues.

Rule: if the visual only works after reading a paragraph, the hook failed.

### 7. Customer-Language Lift

Best when comments, reviews, DMs, support tickets, or Reddit threads are available.

Pattern: use the phrase customers already use, then sharpen it.

Examples:

- `I was not tired. I was fried before the workout started.`
- `The bot did not fail. The handoff did.`

Never invent customer quotes. If unsourced, label as inspired language, not a testimonial.

### 8. Outcome With Constraint

Best for landing pages, tutorials, email, search/social posts, and value-led content.

Pattern: desired outcome + without the thing they hate.

Examples:

- `Catch missed orders without hiring another ops person.`
- `Build the content loop without guessing what people want.`

Avoid impossible timelines, unsupported numbers, or `without effort` claims.

### 9. Teardown / Show-Me Hook

Best for YouTube, Founder Operator, OBS/Excalidraw, and educational content.

Pattern: show a real system, teardown, mistake, or before/after.

Examples:

- `I am going to show you the exact spot where most ecommerce AI agents become useless.`
- `This is why your content calendar keeps turning into random posts.`

### 10. Native Reaction / Social Proof Format

Best for UGC and social-native ads only when the source is clean.

Pattern: clip/pain statement -> reaction -> proof or mechanism.

Do not imply a real doctor, creator, customer, public figure, or third-party endorsement unless sourced and approved.

## Channel Rules

### Landing Page / Prelander / PDP Hero

- Hook must match the traffic source and destination promise.
- Lead with the problem, mechanism, proof, or concrete contrast — not brand poetry.
- A hero hook should make the page worth reading, not try to close the whole sale.
- Include a subheadline if the hook needs context.
- Check offer/product facts before finalizing.

### YouTube Long-Form / Founder Operator

A YouTube hook is not a TikTok one-liner. It should earn the next 60 seconds.

Strong long-form hook includes:

1. Specific operator problem.
2. Clear tension or stance.
3. What the viewer will see/learn.
4. Why Noah has earned the POV, without exposing private No Stranger rows as public proof.

Avoid `If you are in ecom and want to use AI agents...` — that is an audience label, not a hook.

### Short-Form Video / Reels / TikTok / Shorts

First 1-3 seconds need three aligned hooks when possible:

```text
visual hook + verbal hook + text overlay
```

- Start with tension; do not build up slowly.
- Understandable without sound.
- Overlay should be short: usually 3-8 words.
- First visual cannot be generic b-roll.
- Hook must match the next beat.

### Static Ads / Thumbnails / Carousel Slide 1

- 3-8 word headline when possible.
- Visual communicates the thesis before body copy.
- Strong contrast beats clever phrasing.
- No tiny paragraph required to understand the ad.
- Destination page must match the promise.

### Email Subject / Preview

- Subject earns the open; preview pays it off.
- Avoid clickbait that the email cannot satisfy.
- Use curiosity only when the body immediately resolves it.
- For promos, do not hide the offer if the offer is the reason to open.

### Social Posts / X / LinkedIn

- First line earns the second line.
- Prefer a sharp pain/stance/scene over a generic thread starter.
- Do not label sections inside copy as `hook` or `CTA` unless the user asks for annotated copy.
- For Noah/Founder Operator, use public ecommerce pain and real OS work, not private NS rows as public proof.

### Paid Ads

- Generate by angle first, then write variations.
- If performance data exists, analyze winners and losers before inventing new hooks.
- Keep one major variable per iteration when possible: first line, visual thesis, proof framing, audience ID, offer framing, CTA.
- Do not call anything proven unless account data proves it.

## No Stranger Strict Mode

Use strict mode for any No Stranger product, content, paid, organic, email/SMS, landing page, Shopify, creator, or founder work tied to the brand.

Hard constraints:

- Product facts: `500mg KSM-66 + 200mg L-Theanine`, one capsule per day.
- Do not invent testimonials, customer names, founder quotes, reviews, lab tests, certifications, charity claims, or performance stats.
- No fake doctor/creator/third-party endorsements.
- TikTok-facing ad copy: do not use `cortisol` unless a current No Stranger source explicitly approves that placement.
- Avoid retired positioning if current OS files contradict it.
- Humanize before founder-facing delivery.
- Compliance/proof gate beats cleverness.

No Stranger hook sequence:

1. Load `no-stranger-ops-system` and the route-specific reference if needed.
2. Check current brand/audience/performance/source files when available.
3. Apply this hook engine.
4. Apply humanization.
5. Verify product, offer, compliance, proof, and channel fit.

## Generic Kill List

Kill or rewrite hooks that rely on:

- `secret`, `hack`, `unlock`, `game-changer`, `transform`, `revolutionary`, `ultimate`, `wellness journey`.
- `Are you tired of...` unless the rewrite is unusually specific.
- `Unpopular opinion:` when the opinion is normal.
- `This changed everything` without a real object or mechanism.
- `You need to hear this` / `Nobody talks about this` with no specific payoff.
- `High performers`, `busy professionals`, `modern grind`, `optimize your potential`, `default settings`, `before the world gets loud`.
- Fake urgency, fake scarcity, fake stats, fake quotes, fake authority.
- Hooks that promise an outcome the body/page/product does not actually deliver.

## Contradiction Check

Before delivering, ask:

- Does the hook contradict the body, offer, CTA, destination, or product facts?
- Does it imply proof we do not have?
- Does it create a promise the next section does not pay off?
- Does it target one audience while the rest of the asset targets another?
- Does it use a contrarian stance that collapses once explained?
- Does it sound like a different brand/person wrote it than the rest of the asset?
- Would this get worse if read by a skeptical customer, regulator, platform reviewer, or Noah?

If yes, rewrite before showing it.

## Hook Scoring Rubric

Score 1-5 each. Final hooks should usually be 20+/25.

| Gate | 1 point | 5 points |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity | vague category | exact scene/person/object |
| Tension | bland claim | clear pain/contradiction/curiosity |
| Believability | overclaim | sourced or obviously plausible |
| Human voice | AI/corporate | spoken, natural, sharp |
| Continuation fit | bait/slogan | next beat pays it off |

If a hook scores low, do not polish it. Change the angle.

## Output Formats

### Quick Hook Set

Use when the user asks for options.

```markdown
Recommended: [best hook]
Why: [one short reason]
Best use: [placement]
Proof/source: [source level]

Options:
1. [hook]
2. [hook]
3. [hook]

Cut/rewrite:
- [bad hook] -> [why it fails]
```

### Annotated Hook Set

Use for strategy, paid creative, landing pages, or approval handoffs.

```markdown
## Hook Set

### 1. [Hook]
- Placement: landing_page / youtube_longform / static_ad / etc.
- Angle family: [specific pain scene / contrarian reframe / etc.]
- Source pattern: account_signal / customer_language / founder_pov / external_swipe / product_fact / untested
- Why it works: [one sentence]
- Risk: [proof/compliance/contradiction risk]
- Next beat: [what must follow so it does not bait-and-switch]

### Variants
A. ...
B. ...
C. ...
```

### Rewrite Pass

Use when given existing hooks.

```markdown
Keep:
- [hook] — [why]

Rewrite:
- Before: [old]
- After: [new]
- Fix: [specificity/tension/proof/humanization/contradiction]

Kill:
- [hook] — [why it cannot be saved]
```

## Bad -> Better Examples

Bad:
> Unlock calm focus with a holistic stress support formula.

Better:
> One capsule. The studied dose. No proprietary blend.

Bad:
> Are you tired of stress hurting your performance?

Better:
> You did not skip the workout. You showed up already fried.

Bad:
> If you run an ecommerce brand, you need AI agents.

Better:
> Do not let an AI agent run your store. Let it catch the dropped balls humans keep missing.

Bad:
> The future of content is AI-powered automation.

Better:
> Your content calendar is not empty because you need more ideas. It is empty because nothing feeds it.

## Verification Checklist

- [ ] Placement/channel is identified.
- [ ] Hook has a specific person, scene, object, pain, or contrast.
- [ ] Hook creates real tension without fake hype.
- [ ] Proof/source level is clear where relevant.
- [ ] No invented stats, testimonials, endorsements, or authority.
- [ ] No generic kill-list language survived.
- [ ] Hook and next beat/destination do not contradict each other.
- [ ] Strongest hook is recommended, not just listed.
- [ ] For No Stranger: strict mode, product facts, compliance, and humanization were applied.
